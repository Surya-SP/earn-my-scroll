Intentional screen time

Earn your scroll.

Turn walking and focused work into screen-time credits, then spend those minutes intentionally on the apps you choose.

How it works

Earn My Scroll adds a simple exchange between positive actions and distracting app access.

01

Earn

Build screen-time credits by walking or completing focus sessions.

02

Spend

Use your earned balance when you intentionally choose to unlock an app.

03

Relock

When the unlock period ends, Earn My Scroll protects the app again.

Privacy first

No account. No advertising. No analytics or tracking. Earn My Scroll is designed to keep your activity, progress, and wallet data on your device.
