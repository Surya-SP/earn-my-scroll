# Earn My Scroll

**Earn screen time. Stay focused.**

Earn My Scroll is a privacy-first iOS app that turns positive actions such as walking and focused work into screen-time credits.

Users earn time, spend it to temporarily unlock selected distracting apps, and those apps are automatically protected again when the unlock period ends.

## Privacy

Earn My Scroll is designed around local-first processing:

- No account required
- No advertising
- No analytics or telemetry
- No cross-app tracking
- HealthKit step data is processed on-device
- Screen Time and app-protection information is processed on-device
- Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit
